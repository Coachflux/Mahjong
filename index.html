How to Play

🀄
Match Tiles

Tap two identical tiles to remove them from the board.

🔓

A tile must have at least one free side (left or right) to be selected.

⏱
Beat the Clock

Clear all pairs before the timer reaches zero.

🔀
Use Power-ups

Shuffle rearranges tiles. Hint highlights a match. Undo reverses your last move.
